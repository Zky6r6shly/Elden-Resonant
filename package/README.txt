ELDEN RESONANT: Elden Ring x CONTROL Resonant
=============================================

Pick the "Resonant" class (it replaces the Wretch) when you make a new
character. You start with:

  * all six forms of the Aberrant, Dylan Faden's shapeshifting weapon:
    Flurry, Slash, Slice (right hand), Crush, Drill, Extend (left hand),
    each with its CONTROL Resonant combo finisher as its skill:
    Split Pivot, Spin Edge, Wide Edge, Concussive Beatdown, Uppercut Surge,
    Split Orbit
  * seven Resonance powers on your item belt that cost FP instead of
    running out: Force Push, Offensive Shield, Ignite, Inferno, Growth,
    Barrage, Remote Explosion

While you play, the mod reads sounds from YOUR OWN CONTROL Resonant
install (Steam or Epic) and plays them when you shift forms, land a
finisher or use a power. No CONTROL Resonant files ship with this mod.
If CONTROL Resonant isn't installed, the game still works and the
Aberrant's descriptions tell you so.

Melty starts Elden Ring through ModEngine2, offline, with Easy Anti-Cheat off.
Solo only.

Changed vanilla items: the Misericorde, Grave Scythe, Nagakiba, Brick
Hammer, Estoc and Urumi become Aberrant forms; Fire Pot, Redmane Fire Pot,
Giantsflame Fire Pot, Poison Pot, Glintstone Scrap, Gravity Stone Chunk and
Ironjar Aromatic become reusable Resonance powers; the Wretch class becomes
the Resonant.

Log: %LOCALAPPDATA%\EldenResonant\elden-resonant.log

decode CONTROL Resonant's Wwise audio.
